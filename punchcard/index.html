URL Keypunch

Punch a URL onto 80-column IBM cards in EBCDIC Hollerith code, then lay the card over a photo and pin its corners to line it up.

The video plays the deck one card at a time; right-click it to save. Text files are punched one line per card in Hollerith code. Any other file is punched in IBM column binary, 3 bytes across every 2 columns. Up to 2,000 cards are drawn, fewer at full resolution. To save, right-click the image (long-press on a phone) and choose Save Image.
